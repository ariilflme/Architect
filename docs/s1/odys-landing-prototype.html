FAQ

Straight answers.

When does Odys launch?+

December 1, 2026, on the web, iOS and Android. People on the waitlist get access first.

How do you check places?+

Places, opening hours and travel times come from live data sources such as Google Places, and flight status from a flight-data provider. If something can't be confirmed, it's labeled Not verified.

Do you give visa advice?+

No. Visa rules change often and mistakes are costly, so we only link to official government sources.

Can I get my pre-order refunded?+

Yes, in full, anytime before launch. Send us one email.

Will you sell my email?+

Never. We use it to tell you about the launch, and for tips only if you tick the box.
