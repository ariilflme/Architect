Questions

Straight answers.

When does Odys launch?

December 1, 2026, on the web, iOS and Android. The waitlist gets access first.

How do you verify places?

Each place, opening hour and travel time comes from live data (Google Places, transit routing, flight-status providers). The AI organizes your trip; it never supplies the facts itself. If something can't be checked, it's labeled Not verified.

Do you give visa advice?

No. Visa rules change and mistakes are costly, so we only link to official government sources.

Can I get my pre-order refunded?

Yes, in full, anytime before launch. Email us and it's done.

Will you sell my email?

Never. We use it to tell you when Odys launches, and for tips only if you ticked the box.
