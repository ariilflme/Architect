# S1 — 3 short video ideas (Higgsfield)

Format: vertical 9:16, 15–25 s, on-screen captions (sound-off viewing), hook in the first 2 seconds, end card
"Verivoy — the AI travel agent that never makes things up · Join the waitlist · link in bio".
**No generation launched: each one consumes credits → waiting for your go (I'll show the credit estimate first).**

## 1. "My AI planner sent me here" (invented places)
- Hook caption: *"My AI travel planner said this restaurant was amazing."*
- Shots: (a) traveler walking happily down a Lisbon-style street at dusk, phone in hand; (b) arrives in front of a
  brick wall / shuttered, graffiti-covered storefront — freeze on their face; (c) caption "It closed in 2021.";
  (d) phone screen with the Verivoy itinerary: "✓ Verified · Open tonight until 23:00", then a happy table scene.
- Closing caption: *"We check every place against live data. If we can't verify it, we tell you."*
- Higgsfield prompt (shot b): *"Cinematic handheld shot, young traveler with backpack stops in front of a permanently
  closed restaurant with rusty shutters in an old European street at blue hour, confused expression, realistic, 9:16."*

## 2. "It took me 47 minutes to cancel" (subscription trap)
- Hook caption: *"POV: you try to cancel your travel app subscription."*
- Shots: fast-cut comedic loop — "Are you sure?" → "Wait, 50% off!" → "Call us to cancel" → hold music, clock spinning,
  character aging / growing a beard; then hard cut: one tap "Cancel plan" → "Done. No questions."
- Closing caption: *"Honest prices. Monthly if you want. Cancel in one click."*
- Higgsfield prompt (loop): *"Comedic stylized shot, person on a couch staring at a phone as endless pop-up windows
  multiply around them, a wall clock spinning fast, warm indoor light, 9:16."*

## 3. "Gate change at 2 a.m." (copilot)
- Hook caption: *"Your flight just got cancelled. You're asleep."*
- Shots: (a) traveler asleep on airport chairs at night, departure board flips to CANCELLED; (b) phone vibrates:
  "Flight cancelled. Plan B: next flight 07:10, seats available. Refund claim template ready."; (c) traveler wakes,
  reads, smiles, walks calmly while a crowd queues at the desk.
- Closing caption: *"A copilot from departure to return."* (Avoid quoting compensation amounts; say "you may be
  eligible for compensation" to stay faithful to the no-invented-facts rule.)
- Higgsfield prompt (shot a): *"Night airport terminal, traveler asleep across metal chairs, departure board in the
  background flips a line to CANCELLED in red, slow push-in, cinematic, 9:16."*

The in-app screens (shots 1d, 2 end, 3b) should be real UI captures from the landing mockup, not AI-generated, so
the app we show matches the app we ship.
